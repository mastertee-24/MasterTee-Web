MEET THE FOUNDER

Agboola Taiwo Emmanuel

Founder & Mobile Phone Technician

I started my journey into phone repair in 2020, driven by a strong passion for technology and a desire to become an excellent phone technician. What began as a personal skill gradually grew into something much bigger.

I initially started working from home, repairing phones and helping people solve their device problems. As the work grew, I decided to take the next step by moving into a shop and establishing MasterTee Tech Communication.

What I enjoy most about my work is bringing damaged phones back to life and helping customers get their devices working again. Every repair is an opportunity to solve a problem, learn something new, and earn the trust of a customer.

My vision is to build MasterTee Tech Communication into a recognized phone repair brand known for quality, professionalism, and trustworthy service.
